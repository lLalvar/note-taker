DailyMood Journal

Notes and a bit of mood tracking, without the clutter. You write; the app keeps it in one place and lets you search when you need it.

Your data stays in your account. No ads. Privacy and terms are linked below so you can read them anytime.

In the app

Notes

Add and edit notes on your phone. Title, body, and an optional mood per entry.

Search

Find entries by what you wrote, not just the date.

Mood

Tag entries with a mood so you can look back at how things felt.

Trash

Deleted notes go to trash. Restore or permanently remove them when you want.

Settings and sign-in

Themes

Pick a theme that fits your screen. Light and dark are both supported.

Language

English and Russian. You can switch in settings.

Email sign-in

Sign up with email and password. Verification and password reset are available.

Google

You can also sign in with a Google account.

Privacy and terms

How we handle data and what you agree to when using the app are spelled out in the policy and terms. No fine-print surprises.
